About the lab

Intelligence that holds up in the loop.

AI²S is the research laboratory of the Artificial Intelligence and Intelligent Systems group in the Department of Control and Automation Engineering at Istanbul Technical University. We develop theoretical methods in artificial and computational intelligence, and we deploy them on real-time systems: quadcopters, ground robots, autonomous vehicles and industrial processes.

The lab also provides infrastructure and equipment for undergraduate capstone projects and graduate theses.

What drives us

Three questions we keep asking

  1. How sure is the model? Prediction intervals and calibrated uncertainty, from type-2 fuzzy systems to conformal prediction.
  2. Can a human read it? Interpretable fuzzy additive models, fuzzy ODEs and explainable state-space models.
  3. Does it work on hardware? Every method gets a real-time test on drones, robots or vehicles, many of them on video below.
